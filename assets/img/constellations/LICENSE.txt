Constellation illustrations

The drawings in this folder are the constellation illustrations of the "modern"
sky culture in Stellarium (https://stellarium.org), artwork by Johan Meuris, from
https://github.com/Stellarium/stellarium/tree/master/skycultures/modern/illustrations

They are released under the Free Art License 1.3:
https://artlibre.org/licence/lal/en/

For this website they were converted from PNG to WebP; the converted files are
shared under the same Free Art License. The star positions used to place them
(assets/data/constellation-art.json) come from Stellarium's anchor stars and the
d3-celestial Hipparcos catalogue.
