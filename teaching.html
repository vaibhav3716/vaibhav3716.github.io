Teaching

Teaching physics, one idea at a time

What I teach at B.K. Birla College, and the full course notes for Introduction to Astronomy: lecture notes, assignments and resources, free to read and download.

Courses

Assistant Professor (ad hoc), Department of Physics, B.K. Birla College (Empowered Autonomous), Kalyan. Aug 2025 to present.

Teaching is the part of my week I look forward to most. I teach physics to second- and third-year undergraduates, plus an astronomy course open to every year. Each topic starts from the physical idea; the mathematics follows once the idea makes sense, so students see why an equation looks the way it does before they learn to use it.

  2. Semester IV Second year

  4. Semester VI Third year

Astronomy & Astrophysics certificate course

A basic, introductory course in astronomy and astrophysics, open to first-, second- and third-year students.

Student projects

I supervise short research projects for undergraduates, guiding them from choosing a question through method, analysis and write-up. I also help with assessment design and academic mentoring in the department.
